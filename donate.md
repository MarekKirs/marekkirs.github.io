# Support the Lab

Our research on water quality and public health in Hawaiʻi and the
Pacific Islands is made possible in part through the generosity of
donors. Gifts to the lab help fund student research assistantships,
field sampling equipment, and pilot studies that let us respond
quickly to emerging water-quality concerns in our island communities.

## Ways to give

Gifts can be made online through the University of Hawaiʻi Foundation. Please search and select Microbial Water Quality Research (account number 126-9910-4) or Water Quality Proj (account number 120-9710-4) if interested to support water quality research and students involved here:
<p>
  <a href="https://give.uhfoundation.org/campaigns/63435/donations/new" class="donate-button">Give Online</a>
</p>


## What your gift supports

- Student research assistantships and training
- Field equipment and laboratory supplies
- Community water-testing outreach
- Pilot data for new grant proposals
