## Research area 3: Environmental Pathogens...sources, ecology, risk

We are interested in non-enteric pathogens, such as vibrios, *Leptospira*, *Staphylococci*,  and others, that are often naturally present in the environment and pose risk to human helth. Understanding their distribution and ecology helps to manage the risk.
